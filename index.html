Frequently Asked Questions for Sole Traders & ABN Contractors

When do I legally have to register for GST in Australia?
You must register for GST within 21 days of your annual gross turnover reaching or exceeding $75,000 AUD. If you earn under $75,000, registration is optional. Once registered, you must add 10% GST to your invoices and lodge quarterly Business Activity Statements (BAS).
Do sole traders have to pay themselves superannuation?
As a sole trader, you are not legally obligated to pay super for yourself like an employee. However, contributing up to 12.0% of your earnings into super is one of the most effective ways to lower your taxable income via personal tax deductions (concessional contributions cap is $30,000/year).
What are the Stage 3 personal tax cuts?
The revised Stage 3 tax cuts took effect on 1 July 2024 and apply across FY2024-25, FY2025-26, and FY2026-27. They reduce the bottom tax rate from 19% to 16%, and adjust the 30% bracket to cover earnings between $45,000 and $135,000, lowering tax for all Australian contractors.
How does quarterly PAYG work for contractors?
After lodging your first tax return with significant business income, the ATO will enter you into the Pay As You Go (PAYG) instalment system. You will make quarterly prepayments toward your expected annual tax bill so you do not owe a massive lump sum at end-of-year tax time.
